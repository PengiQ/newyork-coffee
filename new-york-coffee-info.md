# New York Coffee (Pakistan): Research Notes

Compiled 8 Oct 2026 from public sources. Items marked **[verify]** were not confirmed on an official page and should be checked with the client.

---

## 1. Who they are

- **Name:** New York Coffee (NYC). Pakistani listings also use "New York Coffee Pakistan" and the Urdu form "نیویارک کافی".
- **Type:** Café / coffee roastery chain with dine-in, takeaway and delivery.
- **Origin:** Founded in **1999 in Bahrain** by two Americans who started a coffee roastery. The first shop opened in Manama and became very popular locally.
- **Parent group:** MIA Holdings (linked from the official site footer). Global company name: New York Coffee W.L.L.
- **Roastery:** Own roasting plant in Bahrain. 100% Arabica beans, roasted locally. Beans are dispatched to stores within 7 days of roasting, and each store only receives 14 days' worth of stock so the coffee is always fresh.
- **Tagline / hero text on the global site:** "Brew Something Inspiring", "Crafting an Extraordinary Coffee", "More than just great, freshly roasted".
- **Vibe:** American-style café. Coffee is served in tall crystal glasses so you can see the layers of caramel, cream and froth. Customers describe it as a calm, cozy, date-friendly and friends-hangout spot.
- **Also offers (global):** Franchise opportunities, careers, wholesale, online shop and coffee subscription.

## 4. Pricing signals

- Third-party blogs list average spend per person around **Rs 425** (coffee only) up to roughly **Rs 1,380** (full meal) in older articles. Treat these as rough and outdated.
- Reviewers describe prices as "a little more expensive" to "normal". Real current prices must come from the client's menu.

## 5. What customers say (useful for tone, not for the site)

**Positives:** Staff are friendly and well trained, the place is cozy and good for dates, menu has photos which helps ordering, coffee served in crystal glasses looks great. Dishes praised: Moroccan Chicken, spinach omelette, Caesar salad, small burgers, Paradise Passion smoothie.

**Complaints:** Some reviews mention slow service, watery coffee, AC not working, smoking indoors, no wheelchair access or dedicated parking. Reviews are mixed, so the website should not quote reviews unless the client supplies approved testimonials.
